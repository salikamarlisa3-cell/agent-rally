How it works

AgentRally reads the official Agent ID registry on BNB Chain. If your address holds an Agent ID, you can check in once per day (UTC). Consecutive days build your streak. Reaching total check-in milestones automatically mints a soulbound badge NFT to your wallet — it can never be transferred or sold, just like your Agent ID itself.
